Latch — Private Gallery

Support and frequently asked questions

Latch keeps photos and videos in a passcode-protected library that lives only on your device, separate from the Photos app. It has no account, no cloud, and no server: nothing you put in it leaves your iPhone or iPad unless you export it yourself.

Getting started

  • On first launch, Latch asks you to choose a numeric passcode of 4 to 12 digits. This passcode is the only way in — see the warning below.
  • Tap Import to copy photos and videos from your photo library into Latch. Originals stay in Photos; delete them there yourself if you want them only in Latch.
  • Organise items into folders and tags, and mark favourites.
  • Deleting an item in Latch is immediate and permanent. There is no trash and no undo, and because the library is not backed up (see below) there is no copy to restore from. Latch asks you to confirm before deleting for this reason.
Two things to understand before you rely on Latch

1. A forgotten passcode cannot be recovered. There is no reset, no email link, and no support override. Latch has no server and no account, so there is nobody who holds a spare key.

2. Your library is not backed up. Latch's storage is deliberately excluded from iCloud and iTunes/Finder backups so that your private library is not copied elsewhere. The cost is that deleting the app, erasing the device, or switching to a new device destroys everything in it permanently. Export anything you cannot afford to lose.

Free and Pro

Latch is free to use, with no time limit. The free version includes the passcode lock, Face ID unlock, the full viewer, folders, tags, favourites, metadata, duplicate detection, and exporting one original item at a time. It holds up to 100 items, 3 folders, and 5 tags.

A single one-time purchase unlocks Latch Pro: unlimited items, folders and tags, the local web server, multi-select bulk actions, image conversion (resize and JPEG), export with EXIF metadata removed, and the storage dashboard. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Reaching a free limit never locks you out of your own data If you hit the 100-item limit, everything already in Latch stays fully viewable and exportable. The limit only stops new imports.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my passcode. What can I do?
Nothing can unlock the library, and that is by design rather than an oversight. The passcode is stored only as a salted hash, there is no server to authenticate against, and the developer has no override. The only way to use the app again is to delete and reinstall it, which erases its contents.
Where are my photos? I can't find them in the Files app.
They are inside Latch's private storage area, which iOS hides from other apps and from the Files app. That isolation is the point of the app. Use Latch's export features to get a copy out.
How do I make a backup?
Use Export to send items to the Photos app or Files, or turn on the web server and download them to a computer. iCloud Backup will not include them. If you are about to change devices, export first — there is no migration path otherwise.
Does Latch change or recompress my files?
No. Imports are stored byte-for-byte as received. Pixels and metadata are only ever modified by the two features you run on purpose: image conversion, and export with EXIF removed. Exporting an original gives you the original bytes.
How do I use the web server?
Open the Web Server tab and turn it on. The app shows an address such as http://192.168.0.12:8080 along with a username and password. Type that address into a browser on a computer joined to the same Wi-Fi network, and sign in with the credentials shown. From there you can browse, upload, download, and run bulk actions.
The web address doesn't load.
Check, in order: that Latch is open on screen (the server stops as soon as the app goes to the background — iOS does not allow it to keep running); that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and not on a guest network that isolates clients; and that you allowed local network access when iOS asked. If you declined, re-enable it in Settings > Latch > Local Network.
I bought Pro but a feature is still locked.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Because Latch is a one-time purchase, restoring works on every device using that account.
Can I use Latch on iPad?
Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad. The library is per-device, though: there is no syncing between them, because syncing would require sending your media through a server.
Is my library encrypted?
Latch relies on iOS's own protections: its storage is in the app sandbox, which other apps cannot read, and the device's own encryption applies while the device is locked. Latch does not add a separate layer of its own encryption on top of that, and the app never claims to.
Do you collect any data about me?
No. See the privacy policy. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network connection to the developer.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by the developer. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
